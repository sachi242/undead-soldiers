Title: Undead Soldiers

Genre: Fast-Paced First/Third-Person Arena Fighter / Dismemberment Shooter

Core Loop: High-speed parkour combat where players do not have standard health bars. Kills require a precise direct strike to an exposed head, while all intermediate weapon fire systematically strips body armor, severs limbs, and degrades mobility without stopping the combat flow.

Game Mechanics & Execution Loop
System	Mechanic	Gameplay Impact
Limbless Movement	Worm Crawling (Torso Wiggle)	Allows players with zero arms and zero legs to slide and wriggle across surfaces at an increased speed.
Emergency Regeneration	5-Second Tombstone Rush	A random tombstone spawns on the map when fully dismembered. Reaching it within 5 seconds fully restores all body parts; missing the window results in instant death.
Hunter Highlighting	Red Outline on Torsos	Opponents stripped down to just a torso are highlighted with a bright red aura through walls, making them easy prey for finishing moves.
Execution Mechanics	Head Stomp / Finishers	Standing over a limbless torso allows for a direct headshot or a close-quarters head stomp execution to secure the kill tag.

Worm Crawling (Torso Wiggle)
When all four limbs are severed, the character drops directly to the chest and uses core momentum to wriggle forward.

Speed & Profile: Because all skeletal mass from the arms and legs is removed, the torso's ground speed increases dramatically (
+40% over standard crawl), allowing the player to rapidly slide toward objectives or cover.

Low Profile: The player's camera drops to carpet level, making them nearly impossible to hit with high-angle gunfire from afar, though they remain vulnerable to vertical drops and area explosives.

5-Second Tombstone Event
Spawn Trigger: The instant a player's fourth limb is severed, a glowing Relic Tombstone randomly spawns somewhere on the map terrain within a reachable radius.

The Countdown: A 5-second timer starts immediately.

Success: Reaching and touching the Tombstone within the 5-second window triggers a full structural regeneration—instantly rebuilding all limbs, restoring body armor, and standing the player back up.

Failure: If the 5-second timer runs out before reaching the Tombstone, the torso suffers a fatal collapse, eliminating the player instantly and dropping their confirm tag on the spot.

Red Outline Highlighting
Tactical Tracking: When an enemy is reduced to just a torso (whether in a prone crawl or worm crawl), they are marked with a bright red aura visible to all opposing players, even through walls and obstacles.

Gameplay Dynamics: This creates an intense dual-sided race: the limbless player is scrambling to reach the Tombstone before the 5-second timer expires, while nearby enemies use the red aura to hunt them down before they can regenerate.

Finishing Moves
To close out the kill on a highlighted torso, players have two direct execution choices:

Ranged Headshot: Fire a single round into the exposed skull using any active weapon mount.

Head Stomp (Melee Finisher): Sprint or wall-jump directly on top of the limbless torso and press the Melee input. This triggers a brief head-stomp animation, crushing the target's helmet/skull instantly and spawning the score tag directly underfoot.
